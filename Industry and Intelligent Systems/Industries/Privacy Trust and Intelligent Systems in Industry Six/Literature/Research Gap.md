# Novelty and competing research

- Industrial adaptive privacy federation has already been explored; SecuFL-IoT (Scientific Reports, 2026): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-11883-1
- Multi-dataset federated intrusion detection, including cross-dataset transfer, was studied in Scientific Reports (2026): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-32567-w
- Edge-IIoTset supports both centralised and federated intrusion experiments: https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3165809
- Bosch's real-production benchmark can test machine-specific shifts and industrial relevance: https://github.com/boschresearch/CNC_Machining

The paper must offer a validated new trust-privacy-utility trade-off, an improvement under industrial non-IID settings, or new out-of-environment evidence. Combining existing privacy/federation terms without distinct technical results will not establish originality. Verify priority claims against updated scholarly literature before writing the introduction.
