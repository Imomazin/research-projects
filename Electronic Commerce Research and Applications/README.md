# Electronic Commerce Research and Applications

## Paper

**Working title:** Causal Attribution in Omnichannel Digital Commerce: A Customer Journey Graph Approach to Budget Optimisation

**Grant output:** P3, UOWD URC26026

**Primary target:** Electronic Commerce Research and Applications

**Paired intelligence build:** Orbit / AI MarTech Growth Intelligence, `Imomazin/ai-martech-growth-intelligence`

## Research folders

1. `01 Research Design` - causal estimands, identification assumptions, graph specification and method freeze
2. `02 Literature` - causal attribution, uplift, experimentation, journey modelling and marketing optimisation
3. `03 Data` - public causal/attribution benchmarks, commerce data and provenance
4. `04 Experiments` - baseline-versus-causal comparisons and validation
5. `05 Manuscript` - held behind build validation for results claims
6. `06 Submission` - journal pack and requirements
7. `07 Build Synchrony` - traceability between P3 evidence and Orbit causal functions

## Operating rule

Orbit is upgraded first from heuristic attribution to a defensible causal layer. P3 then reports the implemented and validated causal system. Manuscript background and method scaffolding may proceed, but empirical claims remain blocked until the causal engine generates reproducible evidence.
