# Literature

Literature work will be organised around:

1. Multi-touch and algorithmic attribution in digital commerce
2. Causal inference for marketing interventions and customer journeys
3. Causal discovery and DAG-based identification
4. Counterfactual campaign evaluation
5. Uplift / heterogeneous treatment-effect modelling
6. Budget and policy optimisation under causal effect estimates
7. Selection bias, ad fraud and measurement error in attribution systems

The review must distinguish predictive attribution from causal attribution and must identify what can and cannot be learned from observational journey data without additional assumptions or experimental evidence.
