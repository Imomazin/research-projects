# Baseline Evidence

The original Paper III 100-seed seed-level record is **Paper III All Results Combined (2).csv**, retained in the Paper III Google Drive research folder. It is the baseline source used to validate the new runner and to regenerate the main-matrix membership figure.

The new extension was checked against that archived record before reviewer-driven experiments were interpreted. The reproduction manifest and runner are in `../06 Reproducibility`.

Baseline evidence should not be overwritten by the new extension. The extension adds severe-imbalance combinations, the stricter clip sweep and the D4 gradient diagnostic while preserving the original Paper III result record.
