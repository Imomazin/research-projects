# ILO GenAI Occupational Exposure 2025

Primary secondary-data source for this project.

**Study:** Gmyrek, P., Berg, J. et al. (2025), *Generative AI and Jobs: A Refined Global Index of Occupational Exposure*, ILO Working Paper 140.

**Official report:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure

**Machine-readable ISCO-08 scores:** https://github.com/pgmyrek/2025_GenAI_scores_ISCO08

The project uses the official occupational exposure categories linked through ISCO-08. Any analytical recoding is documented separately and must not be represented as an official ILO continuous score.
