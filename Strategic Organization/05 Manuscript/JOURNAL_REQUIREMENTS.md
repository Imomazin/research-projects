# Strategic Organization — Journal Requirements

Target journal: **Strategic Organization (SAGE)**

Article type: **Research Article**

Official journal page: https://journals.sagepub.com/home/SOQ

Official submission guidelines: https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/soq

## Scope fit

Strategic Organization publishes discipline-grounded conceptual and empirical research at the intersection of strategic management and organization theory. This paper must therefore be written as a strategy/organization contribution. It must not read as an employability report, training evaluation, HR analytics report or generic AI-readiness study.

## Current formal requirements

- Research articles generally no more than **12,000 words inclusive of abstract, references and tables**.
- Use **double spacing** and **12-point font**.
- Preferred manuscript format is **Microsoft Word**.
- Use an **unstructured abstract of 100–150 words**.
- Provide **at least five specific keywords**.
- Use **SAGE Harvard** referencing.
- Every in-text citation must match a reference-list item exactly in author spelling and year, and every reference-list item must be cited in text.
- Update preprint citations to the peer-reviewed article whenever a published version exists. Preprints are otherwise discouraged.
- Prepare a **separate title page** containing authors, affiliations, acknowledgements and grant information.
- The review manuscript must be **fully anonymised**.
- Tables and figures must be directly pertinent to the argument.
- Number figures consecutively. Figure resolution must be **300 dpi**.
- At initial submission, the manuscript file should contain the entire manuscript. Put each table and figure on a separate page at the end of the submission file and indicate its intended position in the text.
- Use footnotes sparingly.
- A cover letter is encouraged to explain journal fit and contribution.
- A data-availability statement is encouraged, subject to ethical and legal constraints.

## House-style implications for this paper

- Accessible scholarly prose. Avoid jargon that is not doing theoretical work.
- Use terminology consistently throughout.
- Prefer active voice.
- Methods and empirical findings should generally be described in the past tense.
- Do not overload paragraphs with parenthetical qualifications. Put limitations in the appropriate section.
- Do not copy wording, sentence architecture or distinctive phrasing from exemplar papers.

## Word-budget rule for this project

Aim for **10,800–11,500 total words inclusive of references and tables**. This leaves a safety margin below the journal's 12,000-word guidance.

The manuscript body should normally be held near 7,800–8,500 words so that references and table content do not push the file over the limit.
