# Writing Style Blueprint for Strategic Organization

Based on the selected exemplar article by Figge, Anderson and Lewis.

## 1. Opening logic

Start with a contemporary strategic phenomenon, then immediately narrow to what existing research still does not explain. Establish why the omission matters for organizations, not only for technology users. Move from phenomenon to theoretical gap within the first several paragraphs.

## 2. Contribution positioning

The contribution should be framed as a strategy and organization problem. Link individual-level capability patterns to collective or organizational consequences. State clearly which established literature is being extended and what mechanism the study makes visible that prior research does not.

## 3. Literature development

Build theory cumulatively. Each subsection should introduce one conceptual component needed for the final model. Avoid a catalogue-style literature review. Use prior literature to define mechanisms, boundary conditions and competing expectations.

## 4. Research question

Use one central research question that follows naturally from the unresolved theoretical problem. The question should connect the focal phenomenon to a strategic organizational outcome or process.

## 5. Method-to-theory alignment

Explain why the empirical or modelling design is suitable for observing the proposed mechanism. The method should appear as the logical consequence of the research question, not as a separate technical exercise.

## 6. Results narrative

Organise findings around theoretically meaningful contrasts. Present the empirical pattern first, then explain its strategic significance. Avoid reporting statistics without interpretation. Highlight counterintuitive or boundary-condition findings where they alter existing assumptions.

## 7. Discussion architecture

Use the discussion to return directly to the gap established in the introduction. A strong sequence is:

1. restate the unresolved strategic problem
2. explain the main empirical finding
3. identify the theoretical mechanism revealed
4. specify the contribution to strategy and organization theory
5. explain implications for organizational design and managerial action
6. state limitations as theoretical boundary conditions and future research opportunities

## 8. Contribution language

Prefer precise contribution claims such as:

- extends understanding of how...
- identifies a mechanism through which...
- links individual-level capability differences to...
- clarifies when...
- reveals why apparently similar organizations may experience different outcomes...

Avoid inflated claims, generic statements that AI is important and unsupported causal language.

## 9. Paragraph style

Paragraphs are argument-led. The first sentence makes the point. Middle sentences provide theory, evidence or qualification. The final sentence normally advances the argument into the next paragraph. Citation density is high where theory is being established but lighter once the paper is interpreting its own findings.

## 10. Strategic Organization fit for our paper

For the current study, the manuscript should not read as a training evaluation or employability study. It should foreground a strategic organization problem: individuals may possess strong conventional strategic and managerial capabilities while remaining underprepared for occupations with high GenAI exposure. The theoretical task is to explain why this capability asymmetry matters for the organization of strategic work, human-AI task allocation and the development of organizational capability.

## 11. Writing standard to mimic

Mimic the exemplar's disciplined argument structure, theoretical integration, explicit gap-to-contribution movement and restrained claims. Do not imitate or reuse its wording.
