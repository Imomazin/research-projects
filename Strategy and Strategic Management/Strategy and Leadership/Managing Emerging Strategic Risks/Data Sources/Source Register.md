# Source register

| Source | Data | Access | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEC EDGAR | 10-K Item 1A risk factors and XBRL financial statements | https://www.sec.gov/edgar/sec-api-documentation | firm risk exposure, strategic actions, financial outcomes |
| Geopolitical Risk Index | daily/monthly GPR series | https://www.matteoiacoviello.com/gpr.htm | geopolitical shocks |
| World Uncertainty Index | country-time uncertainty | https://worlduncertaintyindex.com/data/ | macro uncertainty |
| Economic Policy Uncertainty | policy uncertainty time series | https://www.policyuncertainty.com/ | regulatory risk |
| NY Fed GSCPI | monthly supply chain pressure | https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/policy/gscpi | operational disruption |
| World Bank Enterprise Surveys | firm survey microdata | https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/ | external validation where comparability permits |
| AI Incident Database | documented AI incidents | https://incidentdatabase.ai/ | technology risk triangulation |
| WEF Global Risks Report | risk perceptions and taxonomy | https://www.weforum.org/publications/ | contextual evidence, not assumed firm-level data |

For each download record source URL, retrieval date, license, source version, hash, coverage and transformations. Check data rights before redistribution.
