# Research design

## Research questions
1. How do multiple emerging risk exposures interact in corporate risk disclosures?
2. Does strategic risk-response alignment predict subsequent organisational resilience?
3. How do dynamic capabilities and ambidextrous strategic responses moderate these relationships?

## Sample
Candidate US-listed nonfinancial firms with 2018–2025 10-K filings. Establish an auditable inclusion/exclusion log and assess survivorship and reporting bias. Do not assert target sample size as achieved.

## Measures
Risk exposure: validated Item 1A NLP measures by geopolitical, technological, regulatory and supply-chain category. Strategic response: separately coded verifiable actions in filing sections and corroborating sources. Risk convergence: co-exposure, interaction and diversification measures. Outcomes: sales growth, profitability volatility, drawdowns and recovery trajectories derived from dated financial data. Controls: industry, firm size, leverage, profitability, year and firm effects.

## Identification
Lag predictors; firm and year fixed effects; clustered standard errors; alternative macro indices and exposures; falsification/placebo checks; missingness and selection diagnostics. Macro indices shared across firms can be absorbed by year fixed effects; identify through pre-specified firm exposure interactions. Do not make causal claims without defensible identification.

## Validation
Human-coded stratified disclosure sample, intercoder agreement, NLP calibration, temporal leakage prevention, out-of-sample tests and documented sensitivity analysis. All numeric results must be computed from archived data and scripts.

## Design as executed (25 October 2026)

The executed design, hypotheses, measures and decision rules are in Analysis/Analysis Plan.md, committed before outcomes were merged with text measures; its Deviations section dates every later change. Ambidexterity is retained as theory only because no reliable firm-level measure exists in these data. Research question 3 above was replaced by the response-coverage hypotheses (H2, H3). The firm-year unit, lagged predictors, firm and year fixed effects and exposure-by-shock interactions were implemented as planned; causal claims are not made.
