# Digital Ambidexterity and Strategic Renewal

**Author:** Imo J. Enang (sole author)  
**Target:** Long Range Planning  
**Study period under evaluation:** 2016–2025  
**Status:** Research design and source feasibility assessment. No empirical findings are claimed.

## Research question
When and how do AI capabilities condition the relationship between digital exploration, digital exploitation and strategic renewal, and how persistent are the resulting performance differences?

## Files
- `Dataset Assessment.md`: source-by-source feasibility, risks, access conditions and selection decisions.
- `Measurement Protocol.md`: construct definitions, competing operationalisations and pilot tests.
- `Pilot Plan.md`: pilot sampling, data checks, decision gates and reproducibility requirements.

## Evidence and research integrity
Do not present data availability, identification, effect sizes or causal results as established before independent verification. Record download date, licence, coverage, missingness, matching rules and version for every dataset. Do not commit licensed datasets, personal information or restricted source files to this repository.
