# Pilot execution plan

## Scope
Proposed approximately 30 US-listed firms across several industries, sampled with pre-specified diversity in size, sector and observed digital activity. Five consecutive years chosen only after source coverage inspection; the full target period remains 2016–2025.

## Tasks
1. Retrieve SEC CIK mappings and verify 10-K and Company Facts coverage for candidate firms.
2. Map historical tickers and firm identity changes.
3. Download filing metadata and test annual-report section extraction and fiscal-year alignment.
4. Test PatentsView assignee harmonisation, patent filing-date alignment and match accuracy.
5. Extract financial controls and industry classifications, logging missingness.
6. Manually code a stratified sample of AI deployment and strategic renewal events, recording sources and coder decisions.
7. Compare proposed proxies for digital exploration, exploitation, AI capability and renewal; test discriminant validity.
8. Produce a pilot coverage table, match-quality table, data dictionary and failure log.
9. Decide whether to scale, narrow the population or change research design.

## Required deliverables
- Firm inclusion/exclusion register
- Source licence and retrieval log
- CIK-to-patent-assignee match audit
- Annual firm-year completeness matrix
- Construct codebook and event evidence ledger
- Feasibility decision memo
- Reproducible code and environment manifest

## Current status
No dataset has yet been downloaded or quantitatively audited in this project. All sample sizes and thresholds are proposals, not achieved observations.
