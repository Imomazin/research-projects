# Warwick DBA triangulation plan

## Identified source
Imowo Enang (2023), *An exploration of organisational ambidexterity implementation*, University of Warwick DBA thesis. Official repository: https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/184708/ . 314 pages, publicly listed thesis PDF.

The public thesis abstract confirms 50 senior-manager interviews and identifies organisational design, market orientation, organisational capabilities and organisational culture as implementation dimensions. It reports quantitative/covariance analyses and the role of dynamic capabilities and organisational culture in organisational ambidexterity and performance.

## Potential use in this paper
- Theory triangulation: contrast archival digital exploration/exploitation measures with DBA organisational-design, culture and dynamic-capability mechanisms.
- Mechanism development: motivate plausible boundary conditions, especially how implementation context enables strategic renewal.
- Construct validation: align coding vocabulary to original thesis definitions without claiming a shared sample.
- Qualitative triangulation: use published, non-identifying findings from the thesis, with explicit citation and acknowledgment that evidence predates the 2016–2025 archival design.

## Not established
Original 408-organisation survey dataset, 75-item questionnaire, full interview transcripts, consent agreements, research ethics permissions and respondent-level data have not been recovered in this project. Those quantities were discussed historically and require verification against original source files before use.

## Research safeguards
Do not merge DBA respondent-level observations into SEC public-company firm-year records without documented firm identity, consent/permissions, temporal compatibility and a justified linkage design. Do not claim the DBA findings are independent validation if the same constructs or evidence are recycled. Distinguish prior published thesis evidence from newly collected archival evidence. Cite prior work and disclose any overlapping data or text.

## Needed originals
DBA thesis PDF, anonymised survey data and codebook, interview consent/ethics documentation, instrument, analytical outputs and any permitted anonymised qualitative coding tables.
