# Measurement protocol (pre-analysis draft)

## Population and identifiers
US-listed SEC reporting firms; assess 2016–2025 availability. Candidate keys: CIK, ticker with historical mapping, GVKEY if licensed, patent assignee harmonised ID. Preserve one-to-many corporate-parent changes.

## Hypotheses for evaluation
H1: Jointly high digital exploration and exploitation is associated with subsequent strategic renewal, conditional on appropriate operationalisation of ambidexterity.
H2: Organisational AI capability moderates this association; direction and mechanism require theoretical justification.
H3: Strategic renewal predicts persistence in industry-adjusted performance, conditional on lag structure and confounding.

## Competing ambidexterity specifications
- Multiplicative interaction of standardised exploration and exploitation.
- Balance penalty based on absolute difference with a separate combined-level term.
- Configurational categories (high-high, high-low, low-high, low-low).
Compare construct behaviour and robustness; do not choose by significance alone.

## AI capability candidates
AI-specific patent activity with filing-year alignment; verified AI deployment disclosures and dated implementation events. Validate with independent manually coded firm cases. A pure keyword measure is disclosure intensity only.

## Strategic renewal candidates
Operating segment entry/exit, material product-market repositioning, strategic acquisitions/divestitures, changes in resource allocation supported by filings and event dates. Build a documented codebook and independent evidence trail.

## Outcomes
Industry-adjusted ROA and operating margin, and multi-year persistence; alternative growth measures and market returns only where defensible and available. Distinguish sustained performance from durable competitive advantage.

## Analysis plan
Document sampling and missingness; plot distributions and time trends; assess firm and year fixed effects with clustered uncertainty; use pre-specified lag models and sensitivity tests. Evaluate selection, reverse causality, measurement error, alternative industry definitions and patent-assignee matching. Avoid unsupported causal language.

## Reproducibility
Version raw source metadata, data dictionaries, transforms, exclusions, random seeds, estimation scripts and a claims-to-evidence ledger. Respect provider access and redistribution terms.
