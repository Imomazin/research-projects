# Research Design

This folder controls P2 scientific scope before results are inspected.

Current core questions:

- RQ1: privacy-utility effects of adaptive per-round privacy budgeting under increasing non-IID heterogeneity
- RQ2: performance of heterogeneity-aware and trust-aware aggregation under benign and Byzantine clients
- RQ3: joint privacy, security, robustness and systems-cost effects when DP, secure aggregation and robust aggregation are integrated
- RQ4: cross-domain generalisation across healthcare and financial fraud settings

The design must remain distinct from Meridian's earlier optimiser/privacy-risk benchmarking work. The new contribution is the integrated cross-organisational architecture and its validated adaptive privacy, trust, robustness and secure-aggregation mechanisms.
