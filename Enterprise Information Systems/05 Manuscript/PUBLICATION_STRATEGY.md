# P2 Publication Strategy

## Primary target

**Enterprise Information Systems**

The original grant identified Systems as the target journal. For execution, the preferred publication route is Enterprise Information Systems, subject to final author approval and any UOWD reporting requirement that specifically mandates the originally named journal.

## Editorial positioning

P2 will be framed as an enterprise information-systems contribution with a validated technical architecture for cross-organisational intelligence under privacy, heterogeneity and unreliable-participant constraints. It will not be positioned as a generic federated-learning benchmark.

The editorial hook is the integration of:

- adaptive privacy-budget orchestration
- heterogeneity-aware federated learning
- participant trust
- Byzantine robustness
- secure aggregation
- reproducible cross-domain validation

The scientific novelty must remain distinct from the existing Meridian/FMLDS optimiser and privacy-risk benchmarking work.

## Manuscript design implications

The paper should foreground:

1. the cross-organisational enterprise problem
2. the architecture and threat model
3. the integrated algorithmic contribution
4. reproducible empirical validation
5. practical deployment implications for regulated digital enterprises

Publication route preference: standard subscription publication unless a zero-cost institutional open-access agreement is available. No APC should be incurred without explicit approval.
