# Literature

Literature work will be organised around five streams:

1. Federated learning under non-IID institutional data
2. Differential privacy accounting and adaptive budget allocation
3. Secure aggregation and cryptographic protection of model updates
4. Byzantine-robust and trust/reputation-aware aggregation
5. Enterprise and cross-organisational governance of privacy-preserving AI

The review must establish novelty against integrated approaches, not simply show that each component exists separately. A structured search log, inclusion/exclusion record and contribution matrix will be added before the related-work section is frozen.
