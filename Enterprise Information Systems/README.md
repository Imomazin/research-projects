# Enterprise Information Systems

## Paper

**Working title:** Privacy-Preserving Federated Intelligence Ecosystems: An Adaptive Privacy, Trust and Secure Aggregation Architecture for Cross-Organisational Machine Learning

**Grant output:** P2, UOWD URC26026

**Primary target:** Enterprise Information Systems

**Lead:** Dr Imo Enang

**Paired intelligence build:** Meridian, `Imomazin/ppdl-cross-org-intelligence`

## Research folders

1. `01 Research Design` - grant alignment, contribution boundary, research questions and method freeze
2. `02 Literature` - systematic positioning and novelty evidence
3. `03 Data` - external dataset registry, acquisition rules and provenance
4. `04 Experiments` - preregistered experiment matrix, results and reproducibility artefacts
5. `05 Manuscript` - manuscript architecture and evidence-linked drafting
6. `06 Submission` - journal requirements and submission pack
7. `07 Build Synchrony` - traceability between paper claims and Meridian code

## Operating rule

No quantitative claim enters the manuscript unless it can be regenerated from a versioned experiment artefact. Raw large, gated or licence-restricted datasets are not committed to this repository; provenance, acquisition instructions, checksums and derived research artefacts are committed instead.
